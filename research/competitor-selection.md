# Competitor selection

## Deep dives

| Competitor | Role in the case | Why it belongs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pollo AI | Multi-model generation platform | Benchmarks Vivideo Manual mode: model choice, text/image generation, settings, output quality, and credit use. |
| InVideo AI | Mature end-to-end Video Agent | Benchmarks Vivideo's prompt-to-complete-video Agent workflow. |
| Revid.ai | Direct short-form/social-video competitor | Tests social-native scripts, generation, publishing, and automation. |
| HeyGen | Avatar and AI-presenter specialist | Provides the specialist benchmark for avatar realism, voice, UGC, and presenter workflows. |
| Runway | Advanced generation and creative-workflow platform | Benchmarks model control, agent behavior, generative quality, workflow depth, and credit use. |

Vivideo is tested first as the baseline, using the same applicable prompts and measurements.

## Capability boundary

The tested Vivideo build did not expose an existing-video editor or long-video clipping workflow. Therefore:

- OpusClip is no longer a deep dive.
- VEED remains strategically relevant but is not a primary product-level comparison.
- Clipping is mapped as a landscape capability rather than forced into the comparable benchmark.
- Timeline editing is treated as a capability gap, separate from first-output quality.

## Landscape-only products

- VEED - editor-first platform expanding into AI generation.
- OpusClip - long-form clipping specialist.
- Zebracat - social-video agent and automation alternative.
- Pika - specialized creative effects and short generative clips.
- Synthesia - enterprise avatar and training-video reference.
- Captions - social-native avatar and automatic-editing alternative.

Do not replace a deep-dive competitor without recording the reason in `research/change-log.md`.

## Runway comparison rule

Separate Runway analysis into:

1. Comparable workflow: first-run agent, text-to-video, image-to-video, advertisement, and avatar results.
2. Product difference: advanced model controls and creative workflows that are strategically relevant but not identical to Vivideo's social-video positioning.
