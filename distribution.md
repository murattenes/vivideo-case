# Distribution and growth mechanisms

Observed during first-hand testing in late July 2026. This file separates acquisition mechanisms from ordinary product features and pricing tactics.

## What counts as distribution

- **Public discovery:** searchable use-case pages, free tools, templates, galleries, and educational content that can attract new users.
- **Referral or affiliate:** incentives for customers, creators, or partners to bring in new users.
- **Shareable-output loop:** exported or published work exposes the product to another audience, for example through a watermark or public gallery.
- **Activation or retention:** quests, reusable assets, prompt guidance, and workflows encourage users to create again. These strengthen growth but are not acquisition by themselves.

A paywall, discount, resolution restriction, or credit pack is monetization. It should not be described as distribution unless it also creates exposure or referrals.
